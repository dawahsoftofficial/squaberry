Recommendation: Six

Six keeps everything that made Drupe work (a berry made of soft squares, a leaf on top) with a third of the parts. It is calm, symmetric and holds up at 16px.

Pop is the same shape with more personality: pick it if you want the logo to feel more playful and to animate on the site.

Three is the most minimal and the strongest at tiny sizes, but leans on the leaf to read as a berry. Seven is the most fruit-like but brings back some of the density you wanted to lose.

Exploration only. The live logo, favicon and share cards are unchanged. Wordmarks are Bricolage Grotesque converted to outlines; the final lock-up would get hand-kerned spacing.
