Recommendation: Drupe, with Quad as the simpler fallback

Drupe is the most ownable: a berry made of soft squares says "Squaberry" without any explanation, keeps the two leaves people already know, and looks digital without looking cold. Its one cost is a separate eight-square version for favicons.

Quad is the most robust: four tiles in the four brand colours work at 16px, animate with one move, and match the site's soft-square buttons. Pick it if you want something you can stamp on anything.

Trace is the strongest in motion and holds up well at 16px, but reads more as an S than as a berry. Prism is the most premium and suits 3D and app icons, but the cube gets busy at favicon size.

Exploration only. The live logo, favicon and share cards are unchanged. Wordmarks are Bricolage Grotesque converted to outlines; the final lock-up would get hand-kerned spacing.
