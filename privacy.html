Who is responsible?

Squaberry is responsible for personal information described in this policy. For privacy questions, contact hello@squaberry.co.uk.

Business identity to add: The full legal name and business address of the data controller were not included in the supplied materials. Confirm and add these details before launch, along with any applicable privacy contact details.

When you visit this website

This website is a static site. It has no enquiry form, account sign-in, advertising pixels or analytics in the files currently provided. It does not set optional tracking cookies.

The company hosting the site may process technical information such as IP address, request time and browser details in server or security logs. What is collected, where it is stored and how long it is kept depends on the hosting provider and its configuration. Confirm those details with your host and update this policy if needed.

When you email us

If you email hello@squaberry.co.uk, we receive the email address and any personal information you choose to include. We use it to read and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss or provide requested services.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary. Email is handled by the email service used for this mailbox, which may process and store messages on our behalf. We retain messages for as long as needed to respond, keep appropriate business records, or handle a follow-up, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers needed to operate the website or email service, and may be disclosed if required by law or to protect our rights. Any providers and international data transfers should be confirmed against the services actually used.

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including appropriate business and legal record-keeping.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to ask about personal information you have sent us, request correction or deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy concern. The rights available to you depend on the data protection laws that apply to your situation. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because the site currently uses no optional tracking cookies, there is no cookie preference panel. If that changes, this policy and the site controls should be updated before those technologies are added.

Updates to this policy

We may update this page when the site, services or data handling practices change. The current version will be published here.
